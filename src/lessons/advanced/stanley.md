The Stanley controller steers for heading error plus a term that pulls harder when the cross-track is large and the speed is low. It uses the front axle, not a lookahead point. The oval under the car is the path it would be compared against.

It is the natural comparison with pure pursuit once localization has given you a pose you believe. There is no starter yet.
